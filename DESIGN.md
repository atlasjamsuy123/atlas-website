# DESIGN.md: Atlas Game Shop website

Direction supplied by the owner (answers given 19 Oct 2026). The agent only transcribed and formatted them.

## Identity

- **Personality:** premium and elegant. Calm, confident, lots of breathing room, few effects that each earn their place.
- **Palette:** Atlas violet, the same purple family as the logo and the Atlas Tools desktop app, on a dark background.
- **Typography:** bold and firm headlines. Big, heavy display type, not the thin light weight the site used before.
- **Motion:** moderate. Scroll reveals and hover transitions only. Nothing that loops forever without a reason.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## System (derived from the answers above)

- **Background:** near-black violet `#0b0915`. Dark is a brand decision: it matches the Atlas Tools app and lets game key art carry the color.
- **Core colors:** violet `#7c3aed` (primary action), lavender `#c4b5fd` (the one accent: key words, focus ring, active states). Neutrals: white for headings, zinc-300 for body, zinc-400 for secondary text (never darker: zinc-500 and below fail contrast on this background).
- **Third-party brand colors:** Tokopedia green and Shopee orange appear only on their own store buttons.
- **Typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans, one family for everything. Designed in Jakarta, so it fits an Indonesian shop; its 800 weight gives the bold headlines, its regular weight stays easy to read in body text.
- **Radius:** 12px for buttons and inputs, 20px for cards and media, full pill only for small chips and the floating navbar.
- **Elevation:** surfaces sit flat with a hairline border. Only product screenshots and the order card get a shadow, because they are the things we want the eye on.
- **Identity motif:** real game key art. The poster wall and the product screenshots are the decoration; no stock shapes, stars, or grids.
